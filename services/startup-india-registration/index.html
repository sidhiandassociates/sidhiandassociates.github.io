Frequently Asked Questions

Is DPIIT recognition the same as company incorporation?
No - incorporation creates the legal entity (Pvt Ltd/LLP). DPIIT recognition is a separate government certification you apply for afterward to unlock startup-specific tax and compliance benefits.
How long does DPIIT recognition take after applying?
Processing times depend on current government turnaround - call us for our current expected timeline based on recent applications.
Does every startup automatically get the income tax exemption once DPIIT-recognized?
No - DPIIT recognition and the Section 80-IAC tax exemption are separate approvals. Tax exemption requires an additional application to the Inter-Ministerial Board and is subject to specific eligibility criteria.
Can an existing (already incorporated) company apply for DPIIT recognition?
Yes, as long as it's within 10 years of incorporation and meets the turnover and innovation criteria - it doesn't need to be newly incorporated.
What are the main benefits of DPIIT recognition?
Section 80-IAC income tax exemption (3 years out of first 10), angel tax exemption, self-certification under labor/environmental laws, easier public procurement, patent fee rebates, and access to government funding schemes like the Startup India Seed Fund.
