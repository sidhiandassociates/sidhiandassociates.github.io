📍 Applicability Varies by State

Professional Tax isn't levied in every Indian state - it applies in states like Maharashtra, Karnataka, West Bengal, Telangana, and several others, each with its own rates and rules. We'll confirm whether it applies to your specific location(s) before registering.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Professional Tax applicable in every state?
No - Professional Tax is a state-level levy, and not every state imposes it. States including Maharashtra, Karnataka, West Bengal, and Telangana levy it, among others, each with different rates and rules. We'll confirm applicability for your specific state(s).
What is the difference between PTEC and PTRC?
PTEC (Professional Tax Enrollment Certificate) is for business owners, professionals, and directors paying PT on their own income. PTRC (Professional Tax Registration Certificate) is for employers who need to deduct PT from their employees' salaries and deposit it with the state.
Do I need both PTEC and PTRC?
If you're a business owner who also employs staff, you typically need both - PTEC for yourself and PTRC for your employees' PT deductions.
What happens if I don't register for Professional Tax where it's applicable?
Non-registration and non-payment typically attracts interest and penalties under the respective state's Professional Tax Act, which vary by state.
How is Professional Tax amount determined?
It's based on income/salary slabs set by each state government, subject to a maximum annual cap (currently ₹2,500 per the Constitution, though actual state slabs and amounts vary).
