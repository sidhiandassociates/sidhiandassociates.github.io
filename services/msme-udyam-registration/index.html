Frequently Asked Questions

Is Udyam registration mandatory?
It's not legally mandatory to operate a business, but without it you can't access MSME-specific government benefits like collateral-free loans, subsidy schemes, and delayed payment protection.
Can a Private Limited Company or LLP register under Udyam?
Yes - Udyam registration is open to proprietorships, partnerships, LLPs, Private Limited Companies, and other business structures, as long as they meet the investment/turnover thresholds.
How long does Udyam registration take?
Udyam registration is a self-declaration-based online process linked to PAN, so it's typically completed quickly once your documents are ready. Call us for our current turnaround time.
Do I need a GST number to register under Udyam?
Not always - Udyam registration is linked to PAN, and GST is only mandatory if your business is otherwise required to be GST-registered.
What are the benefits of MSME/Udyam registration?
Collateral-free loans under CGTMSE, lower interest rates, government tender preference, delayed payment protection (MSME Samadhaan), subsidy eligibility, and easier compliance in several states.
